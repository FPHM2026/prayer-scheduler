Install My FPHM

My FPHM is the prayer minister app — your sessions, your time off, and your messages. Adding it to your home screen makes it open like a normal app, and it's what lets your phone send you notifications.

The addressFPHM2026.github.io/prayer-scheduler/portal/

iPhone and iPad

You must use Safari. Chrome and other browsers on iPhone can't add it.

  1. Open Safari and go to the address above.

    Tapping a link to My FPHM in an email or text usually opens Safari for you.

  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up.

    It's at the bottom of the screen on an iPhone, at the top on an iPad.

  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.

  4. Tap Add in the top right.

    The My FPHM icon is now on your home screen.

  5. Open My FPHM from that new icon and sign in.

    Use the email and password you were given for the ministry. The first time, you'll be asked to choose your own password.

Notifications only work from the home screen icon. Apple doesn't allow a website in Safari to send notifications, so if you skip this step you'll never see an alert. Once it's installed, use the icon from then on, not Safari.

Android

These steps are for Chrome, which most Android phones use.

  1. Open Chrome and go to the address above.

  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.

    Some phones show a small "Install app" bar at the bottom instead — tapping that does the same thing.

  3. Tap Install app, or Add to Home screen if that's what your phone says.

  4. Tap Install to confirm.

  5. Open My FPHM from the new icon and sign in.

    Use the email and password you were given for the ministry. The first time, you'll be asked to choose your own password.

Notifications do work in Chrome on Android without installing, but installing keeps you signed in and puts My FPHM alongside your other apps.

Turn on notifications

Do this once on each phone or tablet you want alerts on.

  1. Open My FPHM from its home screen icon and sign in.

  2. Tap Settings in the row of tabs along the top.

  3. Under Phone notifications, tap the switch to turn it on.

  4. When your phone asks whether to allow notifications, tap Allow.

    If you tap Don't Allow, the switch won't stay on, and your phone won't ask again — see below.

  5. Tap Send a test.

    A notification should arrive within a few seconds. If it does, you're done.

What you'll be told about. A new session booked with you, a change to the date or time, and a session you've been taken off. Every one of those also waits for you in the Messages tab, so nothing is lost if you miss the alert on your phone.

If something isn't working

There's no Settings tab
Pull down to refresh, or close My FPHM and open it again. If it still isn't there, tell the office.
The switch won't stay on
Your phone is blocking notifications for My FPHM. On an iPhone, open Settings → Notifications → My FPHM and allow them. On Android, press and hold the My FPHM icon, tap the i or App info, then Notifications.
The test notification never arrives
Check that your phone isn't in Do Not Disturb or a Focus mode, then try the test again.
I got a new phone
Install My FPHM on the new phone and turn notifications on there too. Each device is separate. Your old phone stops getting alerts once it's gone.
I can't sign in
Your sign-in is the ministry email address you were given, which is not your personal email. Ask the office to reset your password.
